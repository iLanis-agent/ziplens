📦 ZipLens

A ZIP archive inspector that runs entirely in your browser.

What it shows
  • Archive map - every byte accounted for: local headers, compressed data, the central directory, zip64 records, the EOCD, and any junk before or after.
  • Every entry - compression method, packed/unpacked sizes, ratio, DOS timestamp, flags (encryption, data descriptors, UTF-8 names), entry comments, and real byte offsets.
  • CRC-32 verification - stored entries are checked directly; deflated entries are decompressed in-page with DecompressionStream and verified against their stored CRC.
  • Structure warnings - prepended self-extracting stubs, trailing junk, zip64 sentinels, local/central mismatches, unsupported methods, multi-disk spanning.
Why trust it

The parser is checked against Python's real zipfile module: an oracle script derives every expected field (offsets, sizes, CRCs, DOS timestamps, comments) plus content CRCs recomputed with zlib.crc32 over truly decompressed data. 262 checks pass over a corpus that includes stored, deflated, zip64-forced, stream-written (data descriptor), prefix-junk, and trailing-junk archives.
